Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2024

Jucite Reference Manager is built with a "Local-First" architecture. Your data belongs to you.

1. Data Storage

Jucite stores all citations and PDFs locally on your device. We do not operate central servers that collect or analyze your library.

2. Google Integration

If you use our Google Docs plugin or Drive sync, Jucite only requests permission to manage files it creates. We do not access, sell, or share your other Google data.
