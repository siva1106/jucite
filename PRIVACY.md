# Jucite Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 2024

Jucite Reference Manager is built with a "Local-First" architecture. We believe your research data belongs to you.

## 1. Data Storage
Jucite stores all of your citations, metadata, and PDF attachments locally on your own device's hard drive using an SQLite database. We do not operate a central server that collects, hosts, or analyzes your personal library data.

## 2. Google Drive Integration (OAuth2)
If you choose to use our Cloud Sync feature, Jucite will request access to your Google Drive via OAuth2. 
- Jucite only requests permissions to create and manage files that Jucite itself has created.
- Your data is synced directly between your local device and your personal Google Drive account.
- **We do not have access to your Google Drive data.**

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, research habits, or library contents with any third parties.

If you have any questions or concerns, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
